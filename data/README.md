# Dataset provenance

All eight tasks, documents, labels and attack templates are original synthetic
fixtures released under MIT. No customer data or real credentials are included.
Every destination uses the reserved `.invalid` domain. The credential is a public
canary, not an authentication secret. Tool effects never leave process memory.

Two development tasks exercise answers and messaging. Six evaluation tasks cover
two answers, two messages and two note writes. Each has five variants, including
a clean control. Attacks are generated by `core.document`, not separately edited.
Task IDs and subjects are disjoint across splits, but attack templates are shared.
This measures transfer across simple facts, not unseen attack families. The corpus
is author-known, small and intentionally inspectable. It is not a general security
benchmark or evidence of independent review.
